# Changelog

All notable releases of Rivora. Download APKs from [Releases](https://github.com/OmkarPatil-Dev/rivora/releases).

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-06

First public release. 🎉

### Browsing
- Cinematic home with an auto-rotating spotlight, catalogue rows, categories and Continue Watching.
- Quick-preview sheet, full title pages, seasons and episodes, and My List.
- Search with Top Searches and catalogue filters.
- Phone layout and Android TV (remote/D-pad) layout.

### Extensions
- Rivora Provider API v1 manifests and compatible Stremio HTTPS add-ons.
- "Who's streaming?" catalogue switcher; stream add-ons work alongside the selected catalogue.
- Saved titles and history are kept separately for each catalogue.

### Player
- Swipe for brightness (left) and volume (right), hold for 2× speed, double-tap to seek (5/10/30 s).
- Fit / Fill / Stretch, playback speed, touch lock, sleep timer and next-episode autoplay.
- Automatic hand-off to the next source; Open in another app.

### Downloads
- Offline MP4 and on-demand HLS downloads with a dedicated Downloads tab.

### App
- App lock with biometrics or device credential.
- Daily / weekly / monthly update checks against this repository.
- Telegram channel and support chat links.
